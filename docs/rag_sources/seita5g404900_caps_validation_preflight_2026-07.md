## GO / PAUSE / STOP Rules

- GO: published primers and ScrF I digestion are replicated locally, 263A and
  both recurrent parents amplify cleanly, and the assay distinguishes donor and
  recurrent alleles with reproducible bands.
- PAUSE: PCR/digestion details remain incomplete, one recurrent parent is
  monomorphic with 263A, digestion is weak, or heterozygote banding cannot be
  interpreted.
- STOP: assay repeatedly fails to amplify, cannot distinguish 263A from both recurrent parents, or sequencing shows the expected target allele is absent from 263A.

## Evidence Boundary

- This note supports treating the published Si5G404900C CAPS assay as the first
  gate before large crossing investment.
- This note does not prove the CAPS marker is currently ready in the local lab.
- This note does not prove Seita.5G404900 improves lodging resistance in Jingu 21 or Zhangza 13 backgrounds.
- Must verify before use: primer synthesis/order identity, ScrF I digestion
  quality, parent genotype in 263A/Jingu 21/Zhangza 13, heterozygote pattern,
  and at least a small sequencing check if bands are ambiguous.

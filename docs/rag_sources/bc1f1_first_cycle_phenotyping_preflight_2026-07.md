## Intended Population

- Donor parent: 263A.
- Recurrent parents: Jingu 21 and Zhangza 13.
- Target generation: BC1F1 or earliest available F1/BC seedling generation after successful crossing.
- Comparison groups: recurrent parent controls, 263A donor control, CAPS-positive BC1F1 plants, CAPS-negative BC1F1 sibling plants if available.
- Minimum useful sample: at least 10 plants per genotype class per recurrent background for a first nursery signal; larger numbers are preferred.

## First-Cycle Phenotype Indicators

- Plant height in cm.
- Basal internode length in cm.
- Stem diameter in mm.
- Tiller number.
- Heading date or days to heading.
- Lodging score under local nursery or greenhouse conditions.
- Panicle emergence and visible fertility.
- Early vigor or abnormal dwarfing.
- Optional: stem breaking resistance if an instrument is available.

## Genotype Linkage

- CAPS or sequencing call must be recorded for each measured plant.
- Plant ID, cross ID, recurrent background, genotype call, and phenotype row must match.
- Ambiguous marker calls should not be pooled with CAPS-positive plants.
- CAPS-positive and CAPS-negative siblings should be compared within each recurrent background when possible.

## Decision Interpretation

- A short plant-height signal alone is not enough for deployment.
- Early semi-dwarf plants should also have acceptable vigor, fertility, and panicle emergence.
- A first-cycle nursery result can justify continuing to BC2 or selfing, but not direct release or wide deployment.
- If the 263A allele causes severe dwarfing, sterility, or poor panicle development in either recurrent background, treat that background as PAUSE or STOP even if the marker is positive.

## GO / PAUSE / STOP Rules

- GO: at least two CAPS-positive BC1F1 plants are recovered per recurrent background, plants are fertile and vigorous, and plant height is reduced without severe abnormality.
- PAUSE: marker segregation is distorted, phenotype is inconsistent, height reduction is extreme, fertility is questionable, or sample size is too small.
- STOP: no CAPS-positive BC1F1 plants are recovered, CAPS-positive plants are sterile or severely abnormal, or marker calls cannot be linked to measured plant IDs.

## Evidence Boundary

- This note supports a first-cycle phenotyping data structure and decision gate.
- This note does not prove lodging resistance, yield neutrality, or multi-environment stability.
- This note does not replace later BC2F2 fixation, replicated dense-planting trials, yield-component measurement, or multi-season validation.
- Must verify before advancing: genotype-phenotype linkage, fertility, panicle development, recurrent-background response, and later field lodging performance.

# Seita.5G404900 CAPS marker evidence note

## Source Identity

- Source type: literature note
- Title: Foxtail millet plant-height study identifying Seita.5G404900
- Authors/year: Crop Journal plant-height study, 2023
- URL/DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cj.2022.09.003
- Crop/species: foxtail millet (Setaria italica)
- Main trait: plant height, dense-planting architecture, lodging risk
- Relevant germplasm/materials: 263A, Chuang 29
- Relevant genes/QTL/markers: Seita.5G404900, GA20 oxidase, Si5G404900C CAPS
  marker

## Evidence Summary

The local germplasm and KG records describe 263A as a semi-dwarf line used with
Chuang 29 to identify genes controlling foxtail millet plant height under a
dense-planting and lodging problem frame. The records link a single-base
frameshift deletion in Seita.5G404900, a GA20 oxidase locus, with the dwarf
phenotype in two F2 populations involving 263A. The paper reported the CAPS
marker Si5G404900C with forward primer GCTGACCGGCTGATTGTGTTCG, reverse primer
CTCGCGCGGGCACAGGAAG, and restriction enzyme ScrF I. The published assay
reported 263A as a 146 bp band and Chuang 29 as 83 bp and 64 bp fragments after
digestion.

## Breeding-Relevant Claims Supported

- 263A is a documented semi-dwarf donor candidate for plant-height architecture.
- Seita.5G404900 is a GA20 oxidase locus linked to the 263A semi-dwarf phenotype.
- A CAPS marker exists for Seita.5G404900 and can support foreground selection
  planning after local validation.
- The paper provides concrete Si5G404900C primer sequences and ScrF I digestion
  information, reducing the protocol gap from "unknown marker details" to
  "published but not locally replicated."

## Evidence Boundary

- This source does not prove that the 263A allele improves lodging resistance in
  Jingu 21 or Zhangza 13 backgrounds.
- This source does not prove yield neutrality under dense planting.
- Do not infer multi-environment stability or deployment value without BC-line
  phenotyping.
- Primer sequence and restriction enzyme are publicly reported, but local PCR
  conditions, digestion performance, heterozygote pattern, and polymorphism in
  Jingu 21/Zhangza 13 remain to be confirmed before use.

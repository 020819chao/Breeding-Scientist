## Risk Summary

The 90-day introgression start depends on flower timing, fertility, and seed set. The current local RAG notes support a conservative start-up plan but do not contain measured flowering-time data for 263A, Jingu 21, or Zhangza 13 under the same greenhouse or nursery environment.

## Required Observations

- Days to emergence for each parent.
- Days to booting and heading for each parent.
- Pollen availability window for 263A.
- Stigma receptivity window for Jingu 21 and Zhangza 13.
- Seed set per crossed panicle.
- Whether Zhangza 13 is being used as cultivar seed, hybrid seed, restorer line, maintainer line, or sterile-line system.
- Any parent-specific sterility, poor pollen shed, or crossing incompatibility.

## Conservative Start-Up Design

- Sow 263A, Jingu 21, and Zhangza 13 in at least two staggered batches until local flowering behavior is known.
- Keep 263A as the planned donor, but allow reciprocal or adjusted crossing only after fertility and marker interpretation are clear.
- Use Jingu 21 and Zhangza 13 as separate recurrent backgrounds; do not pool them during early evidence collection.
- Record panicle ID, cross date, pollen parent, female parent, operator, seed set, and later F1 genotype.
- Maintain recurrent parent selfed seed as a control and backup pollen source.

## GO / PAUSE / STOP Rules

- GO: at least one synchronized flowering window is available per recurrent parent, hand pollination sets seed, and F1 seedlings can be confirmed by marker or morphology.
- PAUSE: flowering windows miss each other, seed set is low, or Zhangza 13 material identity makes the crossing target unclear.
- STOP: repeated crossing attempts fail, no viable F1 seed is recovered, or material identity/fertility problems prevent a valid recurrent-parent design.

## Evidence Boundary

- This note supports treating flowering synchrony as an explicit 90-day gate.
- This note does not prove flowering-time compatibility among 263A, Jingu 21, and Zhangza 13.
- This note does not prove the 90-day plan can complete BC1F1 production under all greenhouse or field conditions.

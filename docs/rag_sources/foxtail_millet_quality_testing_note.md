# Foxtail Millet Grain Quality Testing Note

This is a seed note for system testing. It links quality-focused breeding goals
to phenotype protocols, marker/QTL clues, and local validation needs.

Breeding target: improve grain quality, protein content, starch quality, and
processing preference in foxtail millet.

Suggested evidence chain:

- Candidate donor pool: quality donor pool.
- Candidate recurrent parents: local consumer-preferred lines.
- Candidate marker route: Quality-QTL preflight panel.
- Phenotyping route: post-harvest quality lab assay.
- Key traits: thousand-grain weight, crude protein, starch profile, color, and
  processing preference score.

Required local validation:

- Confirm seed amount is sufficient for repeated lab assays.
- Pair quality measures with yield and maturity observations.
- Treat candidate QTL claims as exploratory unless local parental contrast is
  confirmed.

Risk notes:

- Quality gains may conflict with yield, maturity, or processing preference.
- Small seed lots and single-environment samples can bias the quality call.

# Rice Breeding Evidence Pack

## Scope

This local evidence card adds a first rice crop pack for the grain-breeding
scientist. It is designed for evidence retrieval and route construction, not
as proof that any named accession is available in the local breeding program.

## Flood tolerance: FR13A, Sub1A-1, and deployment backgrounds

The Nature study identified Sub1A-1 as the primary determinant described for
Sub1-mediated rice submergence tolerance. FR13A is the tolerant donor
background, and the study reported introgression into a widely grown rice
background while retaining agronomic properties. IRRI lists flood-tolerant
derivatives including IR64-Sub1 and Swarna-Sub1.

Sources:

- https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04920
- https://www.knowledgebank.irri.org/step-by-step-production/pre-planting/rice-varieties/item/climate-change-ready-rice

Breeding boundary: genotype the Sub1A target and run a controlled submergence
survival/recovery assay. Do not treat the original IR64 or Swarna record as
evidence that the Sub1A-1 allele is present.

## Drought avoidance: Kinandang Patong and DRO1

The DRO1 study linked a deeper root growth angle to drought avoidance and
reported that introducing DRO1 into a shallow-rooting rice background enabled
better yield performance under drought in the reported context. Kinandang
Patong is therefore a donor reference for a root-architecture route, not a
guaranteed donor for every target ecology.

Source:

- https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.2725

Breeding boundary: measure root angle or depth and grain yield together under
managed drought and irrigated control conditions. Soil profile, maturity, and
genetic background must be recorded.

## Salinity: Pokkali and Saltol/qSKC1

IRRI identifies Pokkali as a salt-tolerant rice reference and describes Saltol
as a salinity-tolerance route used in rice breeding. A linked marker panel can
support foreground and recombinant selection, but marker polymorphism and
donor-segment effects must be checked in the target parents.

Sources:

- https://www.knowledgebank.irri.org/ricebreedingcourse/Breeding_for_salt_tolerance.htm
- https://www.knowledgebank.irri.org/step-by-step-production/pre-planting/rice-varieties/item/climate-change-ready-rice
- https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2020.00833/full

Breeding boundary: seedling salt scores are not sufficient. Continue to
reproductive-stage fertility and yield under a recorded salinity level, with a
non-stress control.

## Architecture: sd1

The rice sd1 semidwarfing gene was positionally cloned as a gibberellin
20-oxidase gene. Earlier mapping work and the cloning study describe linked
RAPD, RFLP, derived-CAPS, and SNP marker approaches. The route is useful for
plant-height and lodging hypotheses, but height reduction must be checked
against biomass, panicle traits, maturity, and yield.

Sources:

- https://doi.org/10.1093/dnares/9.1.11
- https://doi.org/10.1270/jsbbs1951.47.317

## Disease resistance: Pi-ta and Xa21

Pi-ta-mediated blast resistance is associated with compatibility between the
Pi-ta allele and pathogen AVR-Pita/pathotype context. Xa21 genotyping and
inoculation studies support a bacterial-blight resistance route. Neither marker
should be interpreted as universal disease resistance without local isolate
challenge.

Sources:

- https://doi.org/10.1094/PHYTO.2004.94.3.296
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11090207/
- https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02879300

## Grain quality: Wx

The rice Waxy/Wx gene encodes granule-bound starch synthase and is linked to
amylose variation and cooking quality. Wx intron and splice-site polymorphisms
can support parent selection, but the breeding decision still requires
replicated amylose, milling, and cooking-quality measurements.

Sources:

- https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1365-313x.1995.7040613.x
- https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcs.2007.03.003
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8246539/

## Diversity and material access: 3K Rice Genomes and IRRI Genebank

The 3,000 Rice Genomes Project provides public variation and population
structure resources for Asian cultivated rice. IRRI provides a route for
requesting rice germplasm and points users to IRRI GRIN-Global and Genesys for
accession searches. A public genome or passport record is not a local seed
availability or true-to-type confirmation.

Sources:

- https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0063-9
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4035669/
- https://www.irri.org/rice-seeds

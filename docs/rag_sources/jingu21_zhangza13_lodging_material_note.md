## Breeding Role

- Proposed role: recurrent parent or phenotyping check material for lodging and
  stem-strength evaluation.
- Best use case: elite background for testing whether 263A-derived
  Seita.5G404900 semi-dwarf allele improves dense-planting lodging resistance.
- Avoid using for: assuming intrinsic genetic lodging resistance from silicon
  fertilizer response alone.

## Evidence Boundary

- This note supports Jingu 21 and Zhangza 13 as relevant cultivar materials for
  lodging/stem trait management studies.
- This note does not prove they carry favorable Seita.5G404900 alleles.
- This note does not prove they are equally suitable as recurrent parents; Zhangza
  13 may require special handling if it is used as a hybrid or hybrid-parent
  system.
- Must verify before crossing: seed identity, fertility, flowering synchrony,
  Seita.5G404900 genotype, and whether the breeding target is the cultivar,
  inbred parent, restorer line, or sterile-line system.
